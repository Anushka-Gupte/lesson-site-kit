3. Callouts (SPEC Section 5)

Four distinct variants for different pedagogical intentions. 10% accent tint on surface, solid 4px accent left border.

Idea

A GPU is a very fast cook with a helper fetching ingredients from a far-away sabzi mandi. Which one limits you depends on the recipe.

Measured

Measured on 13 November 2026: Pi 1.5.2 fixed cost was 5,231 characters per turn, whereas Hermes Agent 0.19.0 sent 54,120 characters before its first tool call.

Reasoned

For BF16 operations on AMD MI300X: Peak compute (1,307 TFLOP/s) divided by Memory bandwidth (5.3 TB/s) equals an arithmetic intensity ridge of 247 FLOPs/byte.

Caution

Never run untrusted autonomous agent commands without a safety sandbox or brake permission policy active on your file system.

6. Deep Dives & Q&A Disclosures

Math and presenter questions stay folded inside accessible <details> elements so total beginners aren't overwhelmed.

Under the hood: why divide by √d in attention?

When vectors have dimension d, multiplying d independent random numbers causes their sum's variance to scale with d. Without dividing by √d, large dimensions push the softmax into regions with vanishingly small gradients.

Question: Does a language model remember my earlier questions?

No. The language model itself is stateless. The harness re-sends the prior conversation log on every single round.
